ABOUT US

A multi-dimensional services partner.

HISCORP Integrated Services LLC is a multi-disciplinary contractor proudly headquartered in Kismayo, Somalia. Formed by a forward-thinking group of regional and international logistics, construction and corporate operations experts, HISCORP LLC serves as a single-point-of-contact partner for organizations operating across the Jubaland region.

We bridge international compliance frameworks with the practical realities of field execution in East Africa. Through modern project management, direct local supply channels and a vetted network of local human capital, we aim to execute projects safely, transparently and on schedule.

Difficult jobs in difficult places are what we are built for, and we deliver them with discipline and care.

Safety, quality and honesty sit at the centre of our company vision.

MESSAGE FROM OUR CEO

“At HISCORP LLC, we believe organisations working in Jubaland and across Somalia deserve a partner that does exactly what it says it will do.”

Our work supports OCONUS operations, that is, projects Outside the Continental United States, where clients expect US-standard safety, compliance and reporting in remote and demanding locations. Every project we take on, whether construction, procurement, logistics or workforce supply, is built on three values: safety, quality and honesty. We combine deep local knowledge with international standards of compliance and reporting, so our clients can focus on their mission while we manage the realities on the ground.

Our people are our greatest strength. We invest in local talent, work openly with our partners and communities, and hold ourselves accountable for results on every site.

I work on OCONUS (Outside the Contiguous United States) operations, so I understand what international clients expect: clear reporting, strict compliance and reliable delivery, even in remote locations.
