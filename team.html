MESSAGE FROM OUR CEO

Delivering what we promise.

At HISCORP LLC, we believe organisations working in Jubaland and across Somalia deserve a partner that does exactly what it says it will do.

Every project we take on, whether construction, procurement, logistics or workforce supply, is built on three values: safety, quality and honesty. We combine deep local knowledge with international standards of compliance and reporting, so our clients can focus on their mission while we manage the realities on the ground.

Our people are our greatest strength. We invest in local talent, work openly with our partners and communities, and hold ourselves accountable for results on every site.
