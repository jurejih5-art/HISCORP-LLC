Privacy Policy

How HISCORP handles information you share with us.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Who we are

HISCORP Integrated Services LLC (“HISCORP”, “we”) operates this website, hiscorp.so. We are based at Kismayo International Airport, Kismayo, Lower Juba, Somalia. You can reach us at info@hiscorp-gov.com.

What we collect

When you use the contact form we receive the details you type in: your name, email address, the service you are interested in and your message. If you email, call or message us on WhatsApp, we receive the details you choose to send us.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, to prepare quotes or proposals you ask for, and to manage the business relationship that follows. We do not sell your information.

Who processes it

Contact form messages are delivered to us by the FormSubmit email-forwarding service. WhatsApp messages are handled by WhatsApp under its own terms. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, an embedded Google Map on the contact page, and scripts from the unpkg content network, which may receive your IP address and browser details when your browser requests them.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies. If we add visitor analytics in future, we will update this page.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to deal with your request and any resulting business, and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@hiscorp-gov.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
